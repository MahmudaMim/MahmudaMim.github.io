02 About me

Design that's useful first,
beautiful second

Drawing, reading & DIY ✏️

I'm a UI/UX and Visual Designer from Rajshahi, Bangladesh. I work where user research, interface design and graphic design meet — building digital products that are as useful as they are attractive.

Over the last few years I've designed 5+ applications at DappleSoft, delivered contract projects for Digibox, and helped international clients on Fiverr as a Level-2 seller. Today I'm the UI/UX & Graphic Designer at Fiztar, while finishing my BSc in Computer Science & Engineering.

I care about clean flows, honest typography and details that hold up in usability testing — not just in the mockup.
